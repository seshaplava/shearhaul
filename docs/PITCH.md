# ShareHaul — Pitch One-Pager

**Tagline:** Uber Share for intercity freight — Dedicated or Shared — trusted, multi-language, Pan-India.

---

## 1. Problem

India’s road logistics wastes fuel, manpower, and money every day:

- A large share of trucks run **empty or half-empty**, especially on **return trips** (one-way hauls)
- Shippers overpay for full trucks when they only need **part capacity**
- Drivers/fleet owners lose income waiting for the next load
- Coordination still depends on brokers, calls, and WhatsApp — slow, opaque, low trust
- **No reliable price discovery** — same corridor, wildly different quotes; hard to know a fair rate
- **Payment risk** — shippers fear advance fraud; drivers fear delayed or missing settlement
- **Cargo risk / weak accountability** — damage, theft, or delay with unclear POD, claim, or who pays
- **Visibility gap** — “Where is my load?” is still a phone chase; little live track or status
- **Document friction** — LR, GST, and e-way bill handoffs are messy for MSMEs
- **Language / literacy barrier** — many flows are Hindi/English-first; hard for a large share of drivers
- **Time uncertainty** — matching and pickup windows are unpredictable (intercity is not instant Uber)

**Result:** higher freight cost for businesses, lower earnings for truckers, more fuel burn and emissions, and weak end-to-end trust.

---

## 2. Solution

**ShareHaul** is a digital freight marketplace where any shipper can book like Uber:

| Mode | Meaning | Best for |
|------|---------|----------|
| **Dedicated** | Full truck for one shipper | Urgency, high value, full load |
| **Shared** | Multiple shippers share one truck/route | Cost savings, partial loads |
| **Return Load** | Fill empty return trips | Fleet utilization |

**Core loop:** Post load → Choose Dedicated / Shared → Match truck → Live track → e-POD → Escrow release → Rate.

Every shared trip also shows **money saved + fuel/CO₂ saved** — proof of impact.

## 6. V1 product (build to ship)

**Must-have**

- Shipper app: post load, Dedicated / Shared, price, track, pay, rate  
- Driver/fleet app: accept trip, multi-stop navigation, status, POD, payout  
- Matching engine: route + capacity + time + cargo compatibility  
- Escrow payments (UPI first) + basic insurance option  
- KYC for drivers/vehicles + ratings  
- Live GPS tracking + photo/OTP POD  
- Multi-language (top Indian languages) + architecture for all scheduled languages  
- Admin console: verify users, monitor trips, handle disputes  
- Savings dashboard (₹ + litres + CO₂ proxy)

**Explicitly later (not v1)**

- Full broker auctions, warehouse SaaS, cold-chain IoT, pan-corridor AI forecasting, credit line underwriting

## 7. Go-to-market (Pan-India confident, density-smart)

- **Brand & product:** Pan-India + multi-language from day one  
- **Liquidity:** activate corridor-by-corridor (e.g. Mumbai–Pune, Delhi–Jaipur, Chennai–Bangalore, Ahmedabad–Mumbai)  
- **Supply first:** onboard 2–3 fleet partners per corridor, then MSME shippers  
- **Languages:** ship top 8–10 first; keep full i18n for 22+ scheduled languages  

This lets you pitch national ambition without failing on empty marketplace density.
